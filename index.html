💡 How Does This Satisfy the Assignment?

1. What is the Real-World Application?

The Undo mechanism used in apps like Google Docs, Apple Notes, and Microsoft Word.

2. Why is a Stack Necessary?

You cannot undo an action from 10 minutes ago before undoing the one made 5 seconds ago. Rollbacks must happen in exact reverse chronological order.

3. How is LIFO Proven Here?

The Last edit saved is placed at the Top of the stack. When you hit Undo, that top edit is the First one removed (popped).
